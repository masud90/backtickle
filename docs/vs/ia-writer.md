**Short answer:** iA Writer is a carefully designed, paid writing app focused on prose: Focus mode, Style Check and
Authorship tracking. Backtickle is free and open source, runs on Linux too, and is aimed at reading and writing
technical Markdown: math, diagrams, citations and several Markdown flavors. Writers who want the iA experience on a
phone should stay with iA Writer; anyone else who mainly needs a solid Markdown app can use Backtickle for free.

## Where Backtickle is the better choice

- **Free, and open source.** No purchase per platform.
- **Linux.** Backtickle ships a .deb and an AppImage alongside the macOS and Windows builds, all in one release.
- **Technical documents.** Mermaid and Graphviz diagrams, Pandoc citations, Quarto cross-references and callouts,
  and six Markdown flavors detected per file.
- **Accessibility.** Dyslexia-friendly reading, a reading ruler, high-contrast dividers, interface scaling and
  editable shortcuts, checked against WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Where iA Writer is the better choice

- **Prose writing tools.** Style Check and Authorship tracking, which marks typed versus pasted text, have no
  equivalent in Backtickle.
- **iPhone and iPad.** iA Writer has polished mobile apps; Backtickle is desktop only.
- **Design.** iA Writer's typography and calm interface are its signature.

## Switching from iA Writer

Your iA Writer library is a set of plain Markdown files in a folder (local, iCloud, Dropbox and so on). Open that
folder in Backtickle with **Open Folder**. Turn on typewriter scrolling and the focus-line spotlight in Preferences
for an iA-like writing mode.
