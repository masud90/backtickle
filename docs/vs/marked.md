**Short answer:** Marked is a Mac-only Markdown previewer: it watches a file you write in another editor and shows
it beautifully, with strong proofing and export. Backtickle does the previewing too, for free and on macOS, Windows
and Linux, and it is also a full editor, so you can switch between reading and writing in one window.

## Where Backtickle is the better choice

- **Free, with no subscription.**
- **Read and write in one app.** Preview a file, then flip to the Split or Editor view to fix it.
- **Windows and Linux.** The same app on every desktop you use.
- **Flavor detection.** Obsidian notes, Quarto documents and MDX render with their own rules, without setting up a
  custom processor.

## Where Marked is the better choice

- **Proofing and analysis.** Marked's document statistics and proofing tools go far beyond
  Backtickle's word and reading-time counts.
- **Export depth.** Paginated PDF, EPUB, RTF, ODT and more, with detailed styling.
- **Any processor.** Marked can run your own Markdown processor.

## Using Backtickle as a previewer

Keep writing in your usual editor. Open the same file in Backtickle and choose the **Preview** view: when you save
in the other editor, Backtickle picks up the change.
